Presentation Submission Guidelines

Instructions for oral presentations, slides, and self-recorded video submissions for APANConf'26.

Timing of Oral Presentation

  • 12 minutes for presentation.
  • 3 minutes for Q&A.
  • First bell at 10 minutes.
  • Second bell at 12 minutes.
  • Presenters must stop at 12 minutes.

Preparation of Presentation Slides (PPT)

  • Presentation title must match the conference registered paper title.
  • Presenter can organize slides suitably while maintaining clarity.
  • Tables and figures should be clearly visible.
  • Title slide must include paper title, author names, and affiliations.
  • Use the conference PowerPoint template.
  • Recommended file name format: <APANConf2026_PaperID_LastName.ppt>.

Preparation of Self-Recorded Video

  • Maximum duration: 12 minutes.
  • Presenter's face should appear in the top-right corner of the video.
  • Accepted format: MP4.
  • Maximum file size: 25 MB.
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9.
  • Recommended tools: OBS Studio, Zoom, Bandicam, Free Cam.
  • If needed, compress large files (for example with HandBrake).
  • Recommended file name format: <APANConf2026_PaperID_LastName.mp4>.

Submission of Video and Slides

Upload your self-recorded video and presentation slides through the conference submission process.

  • Upload deadline: July 8, 2026 Extended (previously June 28, 2026).
  • Ensure submitted files follow naming conventions and format requirements.

Recommendations and Best Practices

  • Presenters are encouraged to wear formal attire.
  • Use the conference virtual background where available.
  • Choose an environment with minimal background noise.
  • Set camera at eye level or slightly above.
