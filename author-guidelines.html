Author Guidelines

Use this page for APANConf'26 paper preparation and submission requirements.

Paper Preparation
  • The accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore subject to meeting IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements.
  • All papers should be written in English.
  • The conference only encourages the submission of full papers. Maximum length of a full paper is limited to six (06) A4 pages in the given IEEE template format.
  • During the desk review process (prior to the formal double blind review) the scope and formatting of papers will be checked. Papers having non-compliances, including the following, will be rejected.
  • Papers that have more than six (06) pages.
  • Papers that are out of scope of the conference.
  • Papers that do not follow proper formatting guidelines.
  • Papers that have a plagiarism score above 30%.
  • Since the review process is double-blind (neither the author nor the reviewers are aware of each other's identity), authors are requested to follow these instructions.
  • Do NOT include authors' information (name, affiliations, e-mail), acknowledgements, or grant information in the review manuscript.
  • Authors' previous work should be referred to in third person.
  • Guidelines for Generative AI Usage.
  • The developments in generative artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including large language models (LLMs), are transforming publication workflows. We encourage responsible use to support researchers' own capacity to create high-quality scientific work.
  • Authors must comply with IEEE guidelines on use and disclosure of AI-generated content as specified in the IEEE Publication Services and Products Board Operations Manual.
Paper Submission
  • All paper submissions are through EDAS. If you are a first-time user, create an account using your official email.
  • Choose the most appropriate track.
  • Manuscript submission should be in Adobe Portable Document Format (PDF) only.
  • Please contact us if you have any questions regarding your submission or if you experience issues while submitting your manuscript.
  • Conference organizers encourage you to obtain prior clearances/approvals from relevant organizations if ethical clearance is required for your paper content.
