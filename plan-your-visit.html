Visa & Visit

To visit Aotearoa New Zealand you will generally need:

  • A valid passport.
  • A New Zealand Electronic Travel Authority (NZeTA) or a visitor visa, depending on your nationality.
  • A completed New Zealand Traveller Declaration (free; can be done online up to 24 hours before travel).

Most travellers on an NZeTA or visitor visa must also pay the International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy (IVL) of NZ$100.

  • NZeTA: typically processed within ~72 hours; NZ$17. Required for visa-waiver countries, Australian permanent residents, and certain other travellers.
  • Visitor visa: allow 2–8 weeks processing — apply as early as possible.
  • Invitation letter: request during registration, or contact mandy@conference.nz before registering.

August is mid-winter in Auckland (5–15 °C). NZD is the local currency; tap water is safe to drink; emergency number is 111.
